# Yaadio

AI-powered cultural discovery platform connecting people with Jamaican music, events, creators, and experiences.

## Vision

To build the operating system for Brand Jamaica — using technology to help people discover, experience, and engage with Jamaican culture globally.

## What We're Building

Yaadio combines AI, cultural data, and digital experiences to help users discover:
- Jamaican music and artists
- Events and cultural experiences
- Creators and communities
- Future marketplace opportunities

## Current Stage

MVP development and market validation.

## Product Focus

- AI-powered discovery
- Events intelligence
- Creator economy tools
- Cultural technology
